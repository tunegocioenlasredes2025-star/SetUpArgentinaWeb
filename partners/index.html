Partners

Are you a law or accounting firm?

If your clients are expanding into Argentina, partner with a reliable local team. We handle the local execution, company formation, tax, compliance and legal advisory, while you keep the relationship with your client.

What we handle

  • Company FormationIncorporate an SRL, SAS or SA end to end, including your tax ID and, for foreign company shareholders, the Article 123 or 118 registration.
  • Accounting, Tax & ComplianceMonthly filings (VAT, Turnover Tax, Income Tax), financial statements, corporate compliance and payroll.
  • Business & Legal AdvisoryYour ongoing corporate advisor: company law, labor law, contracts and the day-to-day decisions of running a business.
  • Legal Representation & Registered OfficeLocally-resident legal representative and registered fiscal domicile to keep your company compliant.
  • Trademark RegistrationProtect your brand in Argentina: availability search, filing before the trademark office (INPI) and follow-up.

What stays with you

  • The relationship with your client, start to finish
  • Your fee arrangement and your billing
  • The strategic advice on your side of the border
  • A single, named local contact instead of a switchboard
